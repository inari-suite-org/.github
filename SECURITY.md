# Security policy

Our tools exist to defend servers, so a flaw in them matters twice. A way around one of our tools is a
vulnerability, even if it is only possible from inside the thing the tool guards.

## Reporting

Report privately and do not open a public issue. On the repository concerned, open the **Security** tab and choose
**Report a vulnerability**. Include the tool and its version, your configuration, a minimal reproduction, and what you
expected to happen. A failing test is ideal.

We acknowledge reports within 7 days and send a status update within 30 days. Please give us reasonable time to
ship a fix before you disclose publicly. We credit reporters in the release notes unless they prefer not to be named.

Each repository may add its own `SECURITY.md` with a narrower scope. That one takes precedence over this default.
